# Timeless Tiles Digital Marketing Strategy

**Academic demonstration plan — proposed activity only.** Timeless Tiles is a fictional project brand. This document does not report live publishing, advertising, customer activity, or campaign results. No tracking infrastructure is added in this step.

## 1. Executive overview

This strategy connects visual inspiration and practical tile education to the implemented catalogue, room recommendations, comparison, offers, guides, Store Finder, and enquiry journeys. Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and proposed Google Search campaigns each receive a distinct role. Every message should help a visitor move from learning or browsing to an informed enquiry, while making the academic demo status clear wherever commercial or local claims could be misunderstood.

## 2. Marketing objectives

The plan aims to increase digital visibility; attract relevant visitors; improve product discovery and catalogue exploration; encourage engagement with visual tile content; generate product enquiries and quote requests; increase WhatsApp interactions; support showroom discovery; and support eventual sales conversion. These are objectives and expected benefits, not achieved outcomes. Measurement definitions are in [MARKETING_MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md](MARKETING_MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md); no numeric performance targets are asserted without a baseline.

## 11. Lead-generation funnel

| Stage | Channels / platform features | User purpose |

| Discovery | Homepage `/`, guides `/guides`, collections `/collections`, categories `/tiles/...`, recommendations `/recommendations` | Enter the catalogue through an interest or room. |

## 15. Academic-demo assumptions

- Timeless Tiles is a fictional academic demonstration brand; catalogue content and reviews are demo content.
- Offer posts may only reflect existing database sale items, accurately described as demo offers.
- Store Finder entries are fictional and cannot establish geographic proximity or actual retail availability.
- Campaigns, assets, post concepts, ad copy, audiences, and KPIs are proposals/examples, not live activity or measured performance.
- No demographic research, keyword planner dataset, customer counts, audience reach, campaign spend, or commercial outcome is claimed.

## 16. Risks, limitations, and ethical safeguards

- **Pricing and offers:** identify demo pricing; use only current data-backed offer entries; avoid misleading price comparisons and artificial scarcity.
- **Reviews:** never fabricate testimonials; use only approved fictional demo themes, clearly labelled; moderate submitted reviews before display.
- **Claims:** no unsupported superiority, “best,” “#1,” guaranteed, cheapest, or performance/safety claims.
- **Local information:** disclose fictional Store Finder locations; verify every real business address and geographic target before any future local campaign.
- **Lead privacy:** collect only necessary enquiry information, explain its purpose, and follow applicable privacy requirements in any real deployment.
- **Remarketing:** if considered later, use respectful frequency limits and appropriate consent/notice; no remarketing is implemented here.
- **Installation advice:** educational content is not a substitute for site assessment, product instructions, codes, or qualified installation advice.
- **Measurement:** no actual campaign performance can be stated until valid campaign and website data exist.
