# Timeless Tiles Demo Catalogue Dataset

## Scope and fictional status

Timeless Tiles is the fictional demo brand used by this academic project. All catalogue, showroom, and review content is original project content for demonstration only; it does not describe a real tile company, store, purchase, warranty, installation, or certification.

Prices are demo values expressed as **INR per square metre (INR/sq.m.)**. They are not commercial prices.

## Catalogue coverage

| Category | Products |
| --- | ---: |
| Floor Tiles | 8 |

| Outdoor Tiles | 8 |
| **Total** | **40** |

Each product includes original short and full descriptions, a stable SKU and SEO-friendly slug, demo pricing, dimensions, material, finish, colour, application tags, room tags, and stock flags.

## Filter and recommendation data

- Filter attributes: `size_label`, `colour`, `finish`, `material`, `price`, and `applications`.
- Applications: `floor`, `wall`, `indoor`, `outdoor`, and `wet_area`.
- Room recommendation tags: `living_room`, `bedroom`, `bathroom`, `kitchen`, `balcony`, `outdoor`, and `commercial`.
- The dataset mixes Porcelain, Ceramic, Vitrified, Glazed Vitrified, and Stone-look Porcelain with polished, matte, glossy, satin, textured, and anti-skid finishes.

## Product status and promotions

Most products are in stock, with a small number marked low stock, made to order, or out of stock. Featured and new flags provide varied content for future homepage sections. Selected products have a demo sale price that is lower than their regular demo price.

## Stores, reviews, and images

The database includes three fictional active showrooms with demo-only address and contact values. It includes 25 fictional reviews: 24 approved reviews visible through the public API and one unapproved review reserved to verify review moderation.

Product images are intentionally pending in this phase, so `product_images` remains empty by design. The catalogue, search, filtering, comparison, and recommendation website UI are also not implemented yet.
