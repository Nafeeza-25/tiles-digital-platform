The phrase **“best tiles company”** is treated as commercial investigation/research. A suitable educational destination is `/guides/how-to-choose-a-tile-company`; copy should offer objective evaluation criteria and must not claim Timeless Tiles is the best, leading, or number one.

## Match-type examples

- Exact: `[bathroom tiles]`, `[tile quote]`
- Phrase: `"bathroom tiles"`, `"tiles near me"`

These are educational examples for a planning proposal, not a live campaign configuration. Very broad matching is not recommended by default: it can invite less relevant queries and requires sufficient search-term review and account controls. Match behavior can change by platform; verify current product settings before any future launch.

## Negative keyword planning

Illustrative account-level candidates include `free`, `jobs`, `career`, `used tiles`, `second hand`, `wallpaper`, `download`, and `game`. Apply cautiously: `free` or other ambiguous terms may have relevant context, so assess query intent before exclusion. Review negatives against actual search-term reports if a real campaign is later authorized; do not over-block commercially relevant terms.

## Local intent: “tiles near me”

The proposed landing page is `/stores`. The Store Finder contains **fictional demo locations**. They are not verified retail branches and must not be represented as geographically near a real user. Any real local campaign would require verified business locations, accurate geographic targeting, current business information, and appropriate account/location setup before activation.

## Claim and deployment controls

- No “#1,” “best company,” “leading manufacturer,” “cheapest,” “lowest price,” “guaranteed,” unsupported technical performance, or fake urgency claims.
- Use `/offers` only for current data-backed demo offers and identify them as demo items.
- Keep fictional stores and fictional academic brand status clear in local messaging.
- Match every ad to the relevant implemented landing page and review all query/negative keyword choices before any future live campaign.
- This document is a proposal; it does not create accounts, audiences, tags, tracking scripts, or campaigns.
